# KINIKH Group products and services

## Rural products, dairy, fisheries and handicrafts (KINIKH Rural)
Handled by Kinikh Rural and Indigenous Livelihoods LLP (KRIL). Covers farm produce, agriculture products, dairy and milk products, organic food, natural everyday products, fish products, fisheries, aquaculture, handicrafts, handmade and indigenous goods, sourcing from farmers, fish growers and artisans, procurement, processing, packaging, distribution, and rural product wholesale, retail or export. Farmers, fish growers, artisans and rural suppliers who want to sell are also handled here.
- **Team:** KINIKH Rural
- **Email:** livelihood@kinikh.com

- **Team:** Slow Tuesday
- **Email:** stay@kinikh.com

## Websites, software and digital systems (evolKun)
Handled by Kinikh Technologies LLP (evolKun). Covers websites, IT products, business software, business platforms, workflow systems, MIS, CRM, automation, dashboards and digital operating systems. App or ERP enquiries are taken for assessment; the assistant does not claim a particular product is ready or available.
- **Team:** evolKun (Kinikh Technologies)
- **Email:** evolkun@kinikh.com

## Branding, marketing and advertising (BrandWorks)
BrandWorks is a service of Kinikh Technologies LLP. Covers brand strategy, branding, marketing, performance marketing, advertising campaign management, social media, content, campaign execution, events and growth communication. A business client asking about its own ad campaign belongs here. A shopper responding to an advertisement belongs to the team of the advertised product, not BrandWorks.
- **Team:** BrandWorks
- **Email:** brandworks@kinikh.com

## Bills, payments and receipts (Common Accounts)
Common Accounts handles customer financial matters for all KINIKH businesses: invoices, bills, payments, receipts, dues, outstanding balances, reconciliation and salary payment status. Refund payment status is with Accounts, but refund eligibility and approval are with the business team concerned. Accounts has no email address; offer to connect the caller to Accounts.
- **Team:** Common Accounts
