# Routing and clarification rules

## Property, building or stay?
If the caller says only "property", ask: "Is your enquiry about buying land or property, construction work, or booking a stay?" Land or estate goes to Kinikh Landcraft, construction goes to Kinikh Infra, accommodation goes to Slow Tuesday.
<!-- search-terms: property, ghar, house, building, plot or construction, real estate confusion -->

## Packaging
Ask: "Do you need sourcing or packaging of rural products, or packaging design and branding?" Physical rural products go to KINIKH Rural; design and branding go to BrandWorks. Standalone packaging services are not guaranteed.
<!-- search-terms: packaging, packing, box, label, packaging design -->

## Livelihood
Ask: "Is this about selling or sourcing products, receiving support through a social programme, or sharing a livelihood story?" Products go to KINIKH Rural, social programmes go to Empower Society, stories go to People With Purpose.
<!-- search-terms: livelihood, rozgar, income, self help group, artisan support -->

## Website and social media together
A caller who needs both a website and social media or marketing is recorded once for evolKun and BrandWorks together. Ask which need should be handled first; that team coordinates. The caller is not transferred back and forth between teams.
<!-- search-terms: website and instagram, website and marketing, both, digital presence -->

## Caller saw an advertisement
Ask: "Please tell me the name of the product or service advertised, so I can connect you to the concerned team." If they don't remember: "Please describe what the advertisement was about or what you need." Route to the team of the advertised product, not automatically to BrandWorks.
<!-- search-terms: ad, advertisement, saw your ad, facebook ad, instagram ad, vigyapan, offer -->

## Meeting with management
Only an explicit request for a meeting with management goes to the Founder's Executive Assistant. Collect the purpose and preferred date or time. Say the appointment will be confirmed separately; never say a meeting is booked. A complaint, escalation, or a request to speak to a human, a manager or the General Manager is not a meeting request and goes to Central Support. The EA number is never given for any other reason.
<!-- search-terms: meeting, appointment, meet the founder, meet the owner, malik se milna, director, CEO, management meeting -->

## General Manager or a senior person
A caller who asks for the General Manager, the GM, a manager or a senior person to resolve an issue is connected to Central Support, which is the General Manager's desk. This is not a management meeting request and does not go to the Founder's Executive Assistant. Only a caller who explicitly asks to schedule a meeting with management or the founder goes to the Executive Assistant.
<!-- search-terms: general manager, GM, gen mgr, manager, manager se baat, senior, senior person, boss, in-charge, head, escalate -->

## Jobs and HR
Jobs, interviews, joining and employee HR matters go to Central Support for HR coordination until a dedicated HR number is assigned. Salary payment status goes to Common Accounts. No vacancies, selection or interviews are promised.
<!-- search-terms: job, naukri, vacancy, career, hiring, resume, cv, interview, joining, HR, salary, attendance -->

## Refunds
Refund payment status goes to Common Accounts. Refund eligibility and approval belong to the business team concerned (for example Slow Tuesday for a stay). Refunds are never promised.
<!-- search-terms: refund, paisa wapas, money back, cancellation refund -->

## Complaints
Ask which product or service it concerns, then "Please briefly explain what happened." Record it objectively; never admit liability, blame staff or promise a refund. Repeated unresolved complaints, complaints about a team or staff member, and confidential matters go to Central Support, not to the team complained about.
<!-- search-terms: complaint, shikayat, problem, issue, bad service, nobody responded, harassment, misbehaviour -->

## Services not listed
Physiotherapy, medical or any other service not listed is not a KINIKH destination. Route the caller to Central Support for verification; never invent a team.
<!-- search-terms: physiotherapy, hospital, doctor, clinic, medical, school, other service, unknown -->

## Emergencies
KINIKH reception is not an emergency service. For immediate danger the assistant says: "If someone is in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services now. I can also route your concern to the KINIKH team."
<!-- search-terms: emergency, danger, accident, fire, police, urgent, khatra -->
