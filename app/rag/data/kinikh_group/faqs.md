# Frequently asked questions

## What is KINIKH Group?
KINIKH Group is a group of focused businesses covering rural products, stays and retreats, technology, branding and marketing, construction, land and estate development, documentary stories, and a nonprofit for education and welfare.
<!-- search-terms: what is kinikh, kinikh kya hai, about the company, tell me about kinikh, kya karte ho -->

## Can I get prices, availability or a quote?
The assistant does not share prices, discounts, quotations, availability, delivery dates or booking confirmations. It says: "The concerned team will confirm the product details, availability and pricing. I can connect you or request a callback."
<!-- search-terms: price, pricing, cost, rate, quote, quotation, kitna lagega, charges, fees, discount, available, availability -->

## What is the status of my order, booking or project?
The assistant cannot read status. It asks for an order, booking, project or enquiry reference if the caller has one, and says: "The concerned team will need to confirm the current status. I can route your request to them."
<!-- search-terms: status, my order, my booking, my project, update, kab tak, kya hua -->

## What are your working hours?
Working hours are not confirmed on this line. The assistant offers to connect the caller or to record a callback request.
<!-- search-terms: timing, hours, office time, open, band, kab khulta hai -->

## Can I get a team's phone number?
Phone numbers are not shared on the call. The assistant offers to connect the caller to that team right now, or shares the team's email address.
<!-- search-terms: number dijiye, contact only, just the number, email id, give me the number, phone number, mobile number -->

## Can I speak to a person?
Yes. The assistant asks briefly which product or service the enquiry is about so it can reach the right team. If the caller does not want to say, it connects them to Central Support.
<!-- search-terms: human, person, agent, insaan, kisi se baat karao, representative, operator -->
